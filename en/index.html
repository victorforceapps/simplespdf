SimplesPDF

Edit your PDF the simple way.

SimplesPDF is a PDF editor for Android that changes the words already in your document, and the new text looks just like the rest. Sign, fill in forms, merge and organize pages on your phone.

All on your phone, no computer needed.

Sign and fill in

See the app

Your documents stay with you

The app has no account, no login, no ads and no advertising tracking.

On your phone

Opening, editing, signing, filling in, scanning and converting to Word happen on the device, even offline. Your files, photos, signatures and details are not sent to us.

Why the app asks for Google Drive access

Only to convert Word, Excel or PowerPoint files to PDF, and only when you ask. The file goes to your own Google Drive, Google converts it, the app brings the PDF back and deletes the copy there.

Access is limited to files the app itself creates (the drive.file permission): it cannot see or read the other files in your Drive. Nothing goes through our servers.
