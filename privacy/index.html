SimplesPDF Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

SimplesPDF is a PDF editor for Android developed by Victor Carvalho Capanema (victor.forceapps@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Summary

  • Your documents, photos, signatures and personal details stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app has no account, no login, no ads and no advertising tracking.
  • Text recognition (OCR) uses Google's ML Kit, which sends Google technical usage data (never the content of your documents).

What stays on your phone

Everything you open, edit or create in the app is processed on the device, offline:

  • the PDFs and images you open, edit, merge, split, scan or convert;
  • text recognized by OCR (the recognition model is inside the app);
  • the signatures and stamps you keep;
  • "My data" (name, ID number, e-mail, phone, address), which you type yourself to fill in forms;
  • the recent files list and your preferences.

This data is not sent to us or to third parties. It is erased when you remove it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Technical data sent to Google (ML Kit)

The app uses ML Kit, a Google library, to recognize text on scanned pages. ML Kit automatically sends Google, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), information for diagnostics and usage analytics:

  • device information (manufacturer, model, Android version);
  • the app's name and version;
  • a per-installation identifier, which does not identify you or the device;
  • performance metrics, configuration and error codes of the recognition.

According to Google, this data is not passed to third parties. We do not receive it. Learn more in ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Android and Google Play services

  • Fonts for other alphabets are downloaded by the Google Play Store when you ask for them.
  • Read aloud, printing, sharing, camera and file picking use Android's own services and the apps you choose, which follow their own privacy policies.
  • Links in PDFs open in the app you choose (such as the browser).

Permissions

The app only accesses the files you choose to open or where you choose to save. It does not ask for access to the camera, location, contacts or all your files. The internet permission is used by ML Kit (technical data above) and by the Play Store to download fonts.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Since we do not receive any personal data from you, we hold no data to access, correct or delete. Data kept on the phone is under your control. For ML Kit's technical data, see Google. For any question, including under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD, write to victor.forceapps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using data in a different way (for example, in-app purchases), this page will be updated beforehand, with a new effective date.
